Architecture became a systems discipline.

At Carnegie Mellon I study architecture. The project that shaped me most is the Sankofa Bamboo Greenhouse in Homewood: bamboo joinery research as a SURA research assistant, leading a 14-student fabrication team as a TA, and now carrying it toward full-scale construction as a project designer.
